# What this card is

The team's whole weekly rhythm is one forty-five-minute call shortly after each Wednesday session — look back, look at the flock, look ahead (Getting Started Handbook, Section 3). This card is the look-ahead's product: one page, the same grid every week, that says exactly who holds what at the next session. It is filled in live on the call, the Lead Companion reads it back aloud at the close — every person hears their own name and their own line — and the scribe sends it to the whole team before the call ends.

The role names never change; the specifics do. That is what makes the card thirty seconds to fill and impossible to misread.

# The two rules

1. **One name per line.** A role with two names has zero owners. If a job genuinely needs two people, split it into two lines.
2. **The "this week specifically" column is the card.** Not the role name — the specifics. "Cluster Companion" tells Mike nothing; "the ____ and ____ families, and watch the teen who went quiet last week" tells him everything.

Leave a line blank when the week doesn't need it — a blank line is a decision, an unfilled line is a gap. The Lead Companion's read-back at the close of the call is what turns this page from a plan into an agreement.

# Where the specifics come from

Everything in the third column comes from next week's lesson plan, walked briefly in the call's look-ahead: the Quick Reference Card gives the aim, mode, and watch-fors; the Companion Preparation section gives the decisions (option and mode forks, which parent opens, who leads); the Materials checklist gives the print list. The card does not replace the plan — it distills the plan into names.

Heavy weeks — the storytelling weeks' Section 6 re-reads, the Week 19 Dry-Season teaching to the Companions-in-Formation, the Week 21 commissioning logistics — get a **separate working session scheduled on the card**, not a longer call.
