# Practice Hold — Two Weeks, No Meetings

*After Week 6 · the series' seam · re-entry at Week 7*

For the next two weeks there is no session, and the timing is deliberate: the series pauses exactly between its two halves.

## Why the hold is here

Six weeks have done the clearing and taught the hearing: the soils, the knots, the co-processing, confession received and released, and then Week 6's extension of the Scripture-hearing practice to full strength — thirty minutes on a long passage, daily, with a Tell each weekday to someone outside this cohort. What waits on the other side of the hold is the corporate turn: the room learning to hear God as a body. Before a room can hear together, its members need to know — honestly, not hopefully — whether the personal practice holds when nothing is holding them.

So the structure lets go for two weeks, and the question is simple: does the hearing continue when no Wednesday is coming?

## What to carry through the hold

- **The practice exactly as Week 6 shaped it** — extended PROAPT on the passage, daily as you are able, with the Tell going to people outside the cohort. Two weeks of it instead of one. Nothing added.
- **The standing pairs continue.** A pair check-in is not a meeting; it is the structure that travels with you. The pair question stays the same: *what did you hear, and who did you tell?*
- **And carry the room’s question** — keep, change, tell — sent home on no paper at the last session. We answer it together when we return.
- Nothing else. Two weeks of one practice honestly kept — or honestly reported unkept — is the whole assignment.

## What happens when we return

Week 7 opens with the what-held round: the hearing that continued, the hearing that lapsed, the Tell that surprised you, the silence that had something in it. Say it straight — *"I stopped by the fourth day and I know why"* is costly telling, and it counts. Then the corporate turn begins, and you will bring to it whatever two weeks of hearing alone actually taught you. That is the only preparation Week 7 needs.

---

*For the leader: the what-held round opens re-entry before anything else, every report received without correction. And one care note that outranks the calendar: this hold begins one week after confession night. The Companion team checks in personally — a call, a coffee, not a meeting — with anyone whose Week 5 ran deep. Permission is for pages, never for care, and care does not take the fortnight off.*
