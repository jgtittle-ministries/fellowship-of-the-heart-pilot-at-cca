# The CCA Pilot

*Fellowship of the Heart for a school year: three series, walked one Wednesday at a time.*

The CCA pilot is the school-year, family edition of **Fellowship of the
Heart**, prepared for a first **Covenant Christian Academy of Warrenton**
cohort. It is fully written: every session of all three series has its
Companion lesson plan. **It has not yet been run as a cohort.** Its
curriculum is what the [FotH Evening](../evening/index.md) series walks now,
in a home-evening form around a family table, and what the evenings teach
flows back into these plans.

---

## The three series

Walked in order; each builds on what the cohort has already lived.

| # | Series | Length | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [**Getting Started**](../getting-started/index.md) | 22 weeks (a club year) | The Four Connects: Self, Others, God, Mission |
| 2 | [**Going Deeper**](../going-deeper/index.md) | 12 weeks | The diagnostic, therapeutic, and developmental work of IJH Volume 2 |
| 3 | [**Going Out**](../going-out/index.md) | 12 weeks | The body sent: discerning and equipping leaders for new groups |

A cohort that completes only Getting Started has done real work; the
deeper series are for those the Spirit prepares to keep walking.

## What each series contains

- A **landing page** opening the series and laying out the weeks.
- A **Handbook** for the Companion team: the long-form context, the
  theology, the protocols.
- **Weekly Companion Lesson Plans**: Quick Reference Card, WATCH FOR,
  Session at a Glance, Pre-Work, Materials, Detailed Run Sheet,
  Block-by-Block Scripts, between-session practice, and the IJH source
  pointer for each session.
- A **CHANGELOG** tracking what has shifted between versions.

The lesson plans are written for the Companion team, and they are detailed
on purpose. A new Companion preparing for a heavy session should not be
guessing at what to do when the room goes quiet, when a teen weeps, or when
a parent tries to fix what they should be receiving. The page is a floor
under your feet, not a script you read from.

## Cadence and logistics

- **Series length.** Getting Started is a twenty-two-week club year; Going
  Deeper and Going Out are twelve weeks each. With breaks between series,
  the full pilot runs across two school years. See
  [The CCA 2026–27 Dates](../getting-started/cca-2026-27-calendar.md).
- **Session length.** On the school's clock, sessions are 75 minutes
  (Wednesdays 4:15–5:30 PM; the room build takes the first fifteen minutes
  after the 4:00 release). Week 21's family commissioning runs to 6:15. A
  body meeting in the evening will find the plans breathe easily back out
  to ninety minutes.
- **Companion team.** A Lead Companion and a Co-Companion team. Getting
  Started (the family-integrated edition) shares opening and teaching
  whole-room, moves into family clusters (whole families together, parents
  first on sharing practices) for the experiential center, and merges for
  closing. Going Deeper and Going Out keep split-and-merge with
  age-and-role circles.
- **Participants.** Parent–teen pairs, individuals, and households. Each
  series landing page gives its specific fit.

The participant materials the cohort carries, the Personal Heart Journal
and the cards, are in [Shared materials](../shared/index.md).
