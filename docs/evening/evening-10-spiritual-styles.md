# Evening 10 — Spiritual Styles

*FotH Evening · Third movement, Mission and Rhythm · January 2027, two weeks after Heart Bible Study · Anchor: 1 Corinthians 12:4–6*

> **This evening's full page is written before it is walked.** Until then, this page says what the evening does. The school series has no session for it.

*Added at the parents' meeting, 22 November 2026, in The Return's place. The full page and its materials are written before the evening.*

**What it does.** The ways different people meet God, from Christian Schwarz's *The 3 Colors of Your Spirituality*. The inventory is taken online ahead: families may reuse their August results or take it again, and guests take it fresh. In the room we talk it through: our native styles, the styles we find hardest, and the dangers of each. Families lay their Tells and practices beside their styles, and parents name where their teen is strong and they are not.

The evening also does The Return's old work. Evenings 8 and 9 already welcome the room back, so it asks the return questions here: which practice you chose over the break, what held, whether you came back to it on your own, and which you would keep. Then each person restarts one practice.

**Goes home.** A practice in your native style daily, plus one small stretch toward a style you find harder, and the one practice restarted.

**Picks up.** Evening 9's passage, where Eli helps Samuel recognize a voice (1 Samuel 3). When the room took the inventory in August, parents found it showed them how to guide a teen in ways of hearing God they are not strong in themselves.

## Guards

- No style is an excuse, and none ranks above another.
- Profiles are shared only by choice. The group's profile has no names.
- The return questions are asked the way Evening 8 asked them: at dinner, around the table, and one word each in the circle. Slips are data, not a verdict.
