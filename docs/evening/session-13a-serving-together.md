# Evening 13 — Serving Together

*FotH Evening · The room obeys together · Anchor: John 13:1–17; Matthew 25:35–40*

**This evening.** Tonight the room leaves the table and serves someone
together. At *What Was Prepared for You* each of us looked for the works
God prepared for us to do (Ephesians 2:10). Tonight the whole room does
one of them, side by side: parents and teens, with the teens having
planned it. Then we come back to the circle and ask where we saw God.

**Where we are on the map.** The third movement, *Mission and Rhythm*.
At Evening 11, *Doing the Word*, each person took one small step of
obedience. Tonight the room takes one together. Next time, at *The Rhythm
and the Four Questions*, each person builds the rhythm they will carry,
and serving can be part of it.

**Why this evening.** On His last night, Jesus took a towel and washed
His disciples' feet, and then said, "If you know these things, blessed
are you if you do them" (John 13:17). He also said that what we do for
the hungry, the stranger and the sick, we do for Him (Matthew 25:35–40).
The series has evenings for hearing God together and for each person's
obedience. Until now it had none for the room's obedience: the group
doing, together, what it has heard. Tonight is that evening.

**Bring.** Clothes you can work in, and whatever the planning lab asked
your family to bring.

## Choosing what to serve

The planning lab after *What Was Prepared for You* chooses, with the teens
leading the planning and John and a parent alongside. A good choice is:

- **Local and concrete:** someone the room can actually reach this week.
  Meals for a family who is sick or grieving; yard work or a repair for a
  widow or an elderly neighbor; a shift at a church pantry or ministry the
  host church already serves.
- **Something families can do together,** with a job for every age.
- **About an hour of real work,** close enough to drive to and back.
- **Arranged ahead** with the people being served: what they want, not
  what we think they need.

The teens own the plan: who calls whom, what each family brings, who
drives, and the time on site. That is their leadership rep tonight.

**A weeknight or a Saturday.** This page is written for the usual evening.
If the work needs more time, the planning lab may move it to a Saturday
morning instead; the shape stays the same.

## For the two leads

- **Serve with people, not at them.** The people we serve keep their
  dignity. We do what they asked for, the way they want it done.
- **No photos for show.** "Beware of practicing your righteousness before
  other people in order to be seen by them" (Matthew 6:1). A photo for the
  family who asked for one is fine; a photo to post about ourselves is not.
- **No pressure on the people served.** If they ask why we came, say so
  simply. Tonight the kindness is the witness.
- **Safe and covered.** Parents are present; no one works alone or out of
  sight; no ladders, roofs or power tools for teens. The evening walks
  under the same pastoral covering as every evening, named aloud before
  we leave.
- **The circle still matters.** Twenty minutes, even if everyone is
  tired. The work is not finished until the room has said where it saw
  God.

## The take-home (two weeks)

- **Week one:** daily PROAPT, as always. And keep your eyes open: look for
  one more chance to serve someone, unplanned. Take it if you can.
- **End of week one:** the check-in with your partner: *Did a chance come?
  Did you take it?*
- **Week two:** in your journal, two lines: *what I did*, and *where I saw
  God in it*. If no chance came, or you let one pass, write that too. It
  is honest data.
- **Bring it back:** one sentence at the next evening's check-in.
