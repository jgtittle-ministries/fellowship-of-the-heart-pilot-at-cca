# Evening 22 — Sending and Blessing

*FotH Evening · Fourth movement, Consolidation and Sending · 2027 · Anchor: Numbers 6:24–26*

> **This evening's full page is written before it is walked.** Until then, this page says what the evening does, and the full Companion lesson plan stands behind it: [Getting Started, Week 21 — Sending and Blessing](../getting-started/week-21-sending.md).

**What it does.** The family commissioning. Each parent and teen stand together and speak a blessing over each other, and the room speaks the Aaronic blessing over each family (Numbers 6:24–26). The Seeing Round's sentences from Evening 19 can be echoed here.

**Goes home.** The Rhythm Card becomes the long-term practice, until the next series begins.

**Picks up.** All four Connects, gathered into one evening of blessing.
