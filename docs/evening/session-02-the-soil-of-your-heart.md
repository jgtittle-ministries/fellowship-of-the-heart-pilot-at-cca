# Evening 2 — The Soil of Your Heart

*FotH Evening · the opening of the journey*

**The second of two opening evenings.** [Evening 1 — Welcome to the Journey](session-01-welcome-to-the-journey.md)
welcomes the room and opens the map; tonight turns the first box,
*Connect with Self*, into honest self-knowledge. In August 2026 the
founding group walked both in a single evening. From the next running on,
they are two evenings.

---

*Anchor: Mark 4:1–20; Romans 7:15–25*

**This evening.** The seed stated at Evening 1 is recalled, and the Heart
Soil diagnostic from Mark 4, the parable of the Sower and Jesus' own
explanation of it, becomes a frame for honest self-knowledge. Paul's own
mixed ground (Romans 7) is the proof that an honest heart holds all four
soils. This is where *Connect with Self* begins in earnest.

**Bring.** Your notebook or journal, and your take-home from Evening 1.

## The take-home (two weeks)

*Printed sheets are handed out in the room; this is the same content.*

**The standing practice — every day, both weeks.** Morning, five minutes:
"Lord, what are you up to today, and what do you want me to notice?"
Evening, one journal line: "What did I notice today?"

**1. The homework practice — week one.** Three times this week, journal:
where did each kind of soil show up today? No fixing. Just noticing.

**2. The end-of-week-one check-in.** At the end of week one, connect with
the partner you named at commissioning and report how it's going against
the practice. You are not grading each other; you are expecting each other.

**3. The homework practice — week two.** Continue the soil-noticing, or
deepen it; journal what happens.

**4. One sentence of preparation.** Set at commissioning.

*A missed box is never a failure — it is an invitation to return.*

## For the leads

This session's full scripts, watch-fors, and crisis contingencies are in
the school form's [Week 2 Companion lesson plan](../getting-started/week-02-soil.md);
read them before the planning lab. What follows is what the evening form
changes.

- **Question zero stays private.** It is journal-only; nobody is asked to
  share it, and nobody should be.
- **Noticing, not fixing.** The diagnostic names the ground; it does not
  prescribe a repair. Keep the room from turning it into a self-improvement
  plan.
- **All four soils in every heart.** Romans 7 is there so no one leaves
  thinking they are simply "the rocky one." Honest hearts hold mixed ground.
- **Pairs at home scale,** and the merge to the container holder. The
  bright line holds; anything of weight goes to the backstop adult.
