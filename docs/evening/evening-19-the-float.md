# Evening 19 — The Float, with the Seeing Round

*FotH Evening · Third movement, Mission and Rhythm, its close · 2027, before the spring break · Anchor: Proverbs 16:9; 1 Thessalonians 5:11*

> **This evening's full page is written before it is walked.** Until then, this page says what the evening does, and the full Companion lesson plan stands behind it: [Getting Started, Week 17 — The Float](../getting-started/week-17-float.md).

**What it does.** Nothing new is taught. If an evening was missed, it runs tonight. If not, the room walks its Rhythm Cards together, celebrating what held and borrowing ideas (Proverbs 16:9), and each family says its passage again.

Then the Seeing Round.

**Goes home.** One practice, carried on purpose through the spring break.

**Picks up.** The Rhythm Card (Evening 16) and the family passage (Evening 18), and a room that has watched each other for most of a year.

## The Seeing Round

About fifteen minutes. Some growth is visible only to others who have watched over time, so it needs a room that has watched for most of a year. The Float teaches nothing new, so there is room for it here.

- **Two rounds, two sentences.** "What I see in you that I wish for myself…" and "What I've seen you do that I don't think you noticed…"
- **No one chooses whom to affirm,** so no one is left out.
- **Received, not answered.** "Thank you" is the whole response.

It is tried first with the IJH Council on 6 December 2026, and what the Council learns shapes it here. Sending and Blessing (Evening 22) can echo its sentences.
