# Evening 11 — Doing the Word

*FotH Evening · The obey step · Anchor: Luke 6:46–49; James 1:22–25*

**This evening.** Since Evening 6 the room has been learning to hear God:
in Scripture with PROAPT, in the garden, in our honest doubts, and
together in Heart Bible Study. Each of those evenings ended with a Tell,
one sentence of what you heard. Tonight we go back to those sentences and
ask the question the whole journey is about: *did I do it?* Each person
picks one thing they heard and have not yet done, and names the smallest
next step of obedience they can take in the next two weeks.

**Where we are on the map.** The third movement, *Mission and Rhythm*,
opened at The Return. Tonight sits between hearing and being sent: we
have heard (Evenings 6–9), we have come back (Evening 10), now we do one
thing, and next time, at *What Was Prepared for You*, we turn outward to
Mission. Tonight's obedience is small and personal. Mission is the larger
one, and at Evening 13, *Serving Together*, the whole room obeys
together.

**Why this evening.** Jesus put it plainly: the one who hears His words
and does them builds on rock, and the one who hears and does not builds
on sand (Luke 6:46–49). James says hearing without doing is like glancing
in a mirror and forgetting your own face (James 1:22–25). The series is a
curriculum for hearing *and obeying* God, and until now obeying has had a
step (Pray again) but no evening of its own. Tonight gives it one. The
reports at the next evening will be the room's first stories of something
heard and obeyed.

**Bring.** Your journal, with your Tell sentences since Evening 6, and
your take-home from The Return.

## The sentence

> **I heard** ________ (from my journal, at Evening ________).
> **By** ________ (a day in the next two weeks)
> **I will** ________ (one specific thing).

*Small is the point.* Not "be more patient with my brother" but "on
Thursday, when he takes my charger, I will ask instead of yelling." Not
"pray more" but "five minutes before school on Monday, Wednesday and
Friday." A step you can picture yourself doing is a step you can do.

## For the two leads

- **Test before doing.** What someone heard is weighed before it is
  acted on: does it agree with Scripture? Does it sound like the Lord's
  conviction, or like accusation? Nothing that fails the *How Did You
  Know?* checks gets done (1 John 4:1).
- **When it involves another person** (an apology, making something
  right, a hard conversation), the step is talked over with a parent or
  another adult before it is taken. That is wisdom, not delay.
- **No secrets from parents.** A step can be private from the room. It is
  never something a teen has to hide from their parents.
- **No comparing, no performing.** A small step done is worth more than a
  big one announced. If someone can't find one, "I'm still listening" is
  an honest sentence, and they can choose one during the week.
- **Parents first.** A parent says their sentence first in each pair and
  in the merge. Teens learn obedience by watching it, which is the engine
  of this whole design.

## The take-home (two weeks)

- **Week one:** do it, on the day you named. Daily PROAPT carries on
  underneath.
- **End of week one:** the check-in with your partner: *Did you do it
  yet? What's in the way?*
- **Week two:** in your journal, two lines: *what I did*, and *what
  happened* (in me, in someone else, between us and God). If you didn't
  do it, write what got in the way. That is honest data, not failure.
- **Bring it back:** at the next evening's check-in, one sentence: *what
  I did, and what happened.*
