# The Whole Road — Evenings 1 to 21

*FotH Evening · An overview of the twenty-one evenings · First written for the parents' review, 7 November 2026*

> **Download** · [This page (Word)](downloads/the-whole-road.docx)

Twenty-one evenings, about every two weeks, with breaks at Thanksgiving,
at Christmas and in the spring. This page puts the whole plan in one
place: what each evening does, what goes home, and how each evening picks
up what came before. It was written for the parents to review together.
Nothing on it is fixed; the room's comments change it, and the
[change log](CHANGELOG.md) records how.

## The shape of every evening

Every evening has the same frame, so the room learns by repetition.

- **Dinner at 6:00.** The table is part of the evening.
- **The container opens at 7:00:** the same eight steps every time. Safe,
  present, clear, intentional.
- **The check-in circle:** each person reports the two weeks of practice.
  A thin week is an honest answer, and pass is always a full answer.
- **The teaching and the practice:** a short teaching from Scripture,
  then the practice done in the room, often in pairs, a parent with their
  own teen, the parent first.
- **The take-home:** two weeks, four parts. A practice for week one, a
  check-in with a partner at the end of week one, the practice continued,
  and one thing to bring back.
- **The close:** the leaders' feedback round, the container closed, and
  the Aaronic blessing.

Two teens lead every evening, one holding the room and one carrying the
teaching. They prepare with John in a planning lab between evenings.
Parents take part as full members of the circle, and go first.

## Three threads through the whole road

**1. Trust: held, then known, then owned.** The order of the map, Self,
Others, God, Mission, is also the order in which trust grows.

- **Held** (Evenings 1–4). The room learns it is safe to be here: the
  container, the door out named, pass anytime, nothing leaves the circle.
- **Known** (Evenings 3–8). As trust grows, each person lets a little more
  of their inner life be seen, at their own pace: their story, their
  friendships, a place they want more honesty, their doubts. Nobody is
  pushed past what they are ready to share.
- **Owned** (Evenings 9–21). The practices and the room become ours. Teens
  lead the room's hearing, families keep practices at their own table, the
  room agrees and serves together, and each person builds a rhythm to keep
  after the evenings stop.

The hope underneath it all: as we trust each other enough to let our inner
life be seen, we learn to trust God and His Word more (1 John 1:7).

**2. Skills, each built on the one before.** Being present and listening
come first. Then telling the truth about ourselves, then offering safety
to others, then confession and blessing. Only then do we turn to hearing
God: in Scripture, in prayer, testing what we hear, and bringing our
doubts. Hearing leads to doing, first each person, then the whole room
together. The last evenings teach how to keep it going, and how to lead it.

**3. Teen leadership, growing by stages.** Teens lead the container and
the teaching from the first evening. Along the road they take on more:
leading the room's hearing (Evening 9), running the planning (13),
teaching the ways to learn Scripture by heart (16), teaching the mercy
cards (19), and finally being commissioned to lead others (21).

## Evening by evening

### First movement — Connect with Self and Others (Evenings 1–5)

**Evening 1 — [Welcome to the Journey](session-01-welcome-to-the-journey.md)** · *walked in August*\
We open the map, the Four Connects, and set up the container. Each person
says one true sentence about why they are here, and we ask what it means
to be saved (John 10:10; Romans 10:9–10).\
*Goes home:* the two daily questions. Morning: "Lord, what are you up to
today, and what do you want me to notice?" Evening: "What did I notice
today?"\
*Builds:* the frame every later evening stands on.

**Evening 2 — [The Soil of Your Heart](session-02-the-soil-of-your-heart.md)** · *walked in August*\
The four soils of Mark 4, and Paul's divided ground (Romans 7). In pairs,
each person looks honestly at their own heart. No fixing.\
*Goes home:* three times a week, journal where each soil showed up that day.\
*Picks up:* Evening 1's noticing, now turned on our own heart.

**Evening 3 — [Telling Your Story](session-03-04-telling-your-story.md)** · *walked in August*\
One question, one circle, and everyone tells: parents first, then teens at
their own depth. After each telling, silence and a short blessing. Nothing
leaves the circle (Psalm 139).\
*Goes home:* Joint Footprints. Each parent and teen walk each other
through their story, twenty minutes.\
*Picks up:* the safety of the container, used for the first time to be
known. Trust begins to move from held to known.

**Evening 4 — [Knowing and Being Known](session-05-knowing-and-being-known.md)** · *walked 15 September*\
The four conditions of the container, taught properly (Ecclesiastes
4:9–12), then applied to our real friendships with a friendship map.\
*Goes home:* choose one friendship, and offer one condition there a little
more than usual.\
*Picks up:* what the room has felt in the container, now carried into
friendships outside it.

**Evening 5 — [Safe and Brave Together](session-06-safe-and-brave-together.md)** · *walked 7 October*\
Confession and restoration, age-appropriate (James 5:16; 1 John 1:9). Each
person names one place they want to walk in more honesty, the place and
not the details, and the circle blesses them.\
*Goes home:* the five-minute Examen each evening, and three questions
about the group (The Road So Far).\
*Picks up:* the safety built in Evenings 1–4. It is the deepest point of
being known so far, and it closes the first movement.

### Second movement — Connect with God (Evenings 6–9)

**Evening 6 — [Hearing God in Scripture](session-07-08-hearing-god-in-scripture.md)** · *walked 26 October*\
The room first answers the three questions about itself. Then PROAPT, the
practice of hearing God in a passage, walked twice: together, then in
family pairs.\
*Goes home:* PROAPT one paragraph a day, ending with the Tell: one
sentence of what I heard, said to someone.\
*Picks up:* the morning question, "what are you up to?", becomes a daily
reading practice. The trust built with each other now turns toward God
and His Word.

**Evening 7 — [The Garden of Your Heart](session-09-10-the-garden-of-your-heart.md)** · *Tuesday 10 November, next*\
We remember a real time we heard God, and ask how we knew. Then a guided
time of prayer: picturing the heart as a garden and inviting Jesus in.
Notice, don't conclude.\
*Goes home:* return to the garden three times, with two checks: does it
fit Scripture, and who could I tell? Then, over the Thanksgiving gap, one
practice chosen from The Practices So Far.\
*Picks up:* Evening 2's heart soil and Evening 6's hearing. It adds the
first testing of what we hear: How Did You Know?

**Evening 8 — [Any Doubts?](session-11-any-doubts.md)** · *Tuesday 1 or 8 December, expected depending on family situations*\
First the return: what held over four weeks apart. Then naming honest
doubts to God, in parent–teen pairs, the parent first ("I believe; help my
unbelief!", Mark 9:24). Then the silent place: a time God seemed silent,
with a time we did hear Him brought in.\
*Goes home:* a private doubts inventory, two journal questions, and over
Christmas one chosen practice a week.\
*Picks up:* being known (Evenings 3–5), now with God: honesty about faith
itself. The testing from Evening 7 returns.

**Evening 9 — [Heart Bible Study](session-11a-heart-bible-study.md)** · *January*\
Welcome home after Christmas. This is PROAPT in its group form: the room hears God
together in one passage (1 Samuel 3), and a teen leads the room's hearing.\
*Goes home:* daily PROAPT, and in week two one Heart Bible Study at your
own family table, a teen leading.\
*Picks up:* Evening 6's personal practice becomes the room's practice and
the family's. Trust begins to be owned: teens lead the room and the table.

### Third movement — Mission and Rhythm (Evenings 10–17)

**Evening 10 — [The Return](../getting-started/week-12-return.md)**\
We rebuild the room after the break, read honestly what held and what
slipped, and re-anchor the year (Lamentations 3:22–23). Slips are data,
not failure.\
*Goes home:* restart one daily practice, just one, and find and mark your
Tell sentences from Evenings 6–9.\
*Picks up:* every practice so far, chosen from rather than all at once.

**Evening 11 — [Doing the Word](session-12a-doing-the-word.md)**\
The obey step. Each person reads back what they have heard since Evening 6,
picks one thing not yet done, and names the smallest next step (Luke
6:46–49; James 1:22–25). It is tested first, then shared in parent–teen
pairs.\
*Goes home:* do it on the day named, then two journal lines: what I did,
and what happened.\
*Picks up:* everything heard in Evenings 6–9, and the testing from 7–8.
Hearing becomes doing.

**Evening 12 — [What Was Prepared for You](../getting-started/week-13-mission.md)**\
Mission begins: the good works God prepared for each of us (Ephesians
2:10). Our gifts, and the difference between what I do and who I am when I
show up at my best.\
*Goes home:* one small action that fits what you heard, and an idea of
someone the room could serve together.\
*Picks up:* Evening 11's one step, now set inside a calling.

**Evening 13 — [Serving Together](session-13a-serving-together.md)**\
The room's obedience, and three skills a group needs: hearing God
together, planning together and agreeing together (John 13:17; Acts
13:1–3; Acts 15:28). Teens run the planning. Agreement is not a vote, and
the room may pass if it is not of one mind.\
*Goes home:* the room serves someone together in the two weeks, then
reports back. If the room passed: eyes open for one chance to serve, and
the question, what kept us from one mind?\
*Picks up:* each person's obedience (11) and calling (12) become the
room's. Whether the room agrees or passes tells us how far the group has
become one.

**Evening 14 — [The Rhythm and the Four Questions](../getting-started/week-15-rhythm.md)**\
The serving report opens the evening. Then each person builds a Rhythm
Card: the practices they will keep after the evenings stop (Galatians
6:9). Parents receive four questions to ask their teen at home, if they
are willing, and to hear the answers without defending (Psalm 139:23–24).\
*Goes home:* run the Rhythm Card for real; for willing parents, the four
questions.\
*Picks up:* every practice so far, now chosen and arranged into a personal
rhythm. Evening 3's prayer, "Search me, O God," is now asked between
parent and teen.

**Evening 15 — [Family Conversation Night](../getting-started/week-16-family-night.md)**\
Low teaching, high warmth: a shared meal, a table grace (Acts 2:46), and
conversation cards over dessert. The container simply holds us for an
evening.\
*Goes home:* keep the Rhythm Card, and talk as a family about a passage to
learn by heart.\
*Picks up:* the trust the room has built, enjoyed for its own sake.

**Evening 16 — [Carrying the Word](session-16a-carrying-the-word.md)**\
Each family chooses a short passage and starts learning it by heart
together (Deuteronomy 6:6–9; Psalm 119:11). The teens teach five ways to
carry it, and each family says its passage to the room.\
*Goes home:* say the passage together, as a family, every day.\
*Picks up:* hearing the Word (PROAPT, Heart Bible Study) becomes carrying
it, at home, in ordinary moments.

**Evening 17 — [The Float](../getting-started/week-17-float.md)**\
Nothing new is taught. If an evening was missed, it runs tonight. If not,
the room walks its Rhythm Cards together, celebrating what held and
borrowing ideas (Proverbs 16:9). Each family says its passage again.\
*Goes home:* one practice, carried on purpose through the spring break.\
*Picks up:* the Rhythm Card (14) and the family passage (16).

### Fourth movement — Consolidation and Sending (Evenings 18–21)

**Evening 18 — [The Return II](../getting-started/week-18-return-2.md)**\
A light return. The room knows how to come back now, and carries its own
return (Galatians 6:9; Philippians 1:6). Each family says its passage once
more, and the final stretch is set.\
*Goes home:* keep the rhythm, and pray over a list of people you might
invite to the next series.\
*Picks up:* the returns of Evenings 8 and 10, now led by the room itself.

**Evening 19 — [The Long Walk](../getting-started/week-20-long-walk.md)**\
Teens teach the room two mercy cards: the signs of drifting, and the path
home. Then the room walks back through its own year, journal in hand, and
gathers one-sentence testimonies (Isaiah 50:10).\
*Goes home:* find the two cards at home, and show one family member where
they live.\
*Picks up:* the whole year, read back in the journal. The teens teach the
room.

**Evening 20 — [Sending and Blessing](../getting-started/week-21-sending.md)**\
The family commissioning. Each parent and teen stand together and speak a
blessing over each other, and the room speaks the Aaronic blessing over
each family (Numbers 6:24–26).\
*Goes home:* the Rhythm Card becomes the long-term practice, until the
next series begins.\
*Picks up:* all four Connects, gathered into one evening of blessing.

**Evening 21 — [Commissioning the Companions](../getting-started/week-22-commissioning.md)**\
The teens who have led are commissioned to lead others (2 Timothy 2:2;
1 Timothy 4:12). They open and close the container from memory, the
covering is named, and their first planning lab for leading others is set
before anyone leaves.\
*Goes home:* for the commissioned, the first lab; for everyone, the rhythm.\
*Picks up:* twenty evenings of leading, now handed on. Trust, fully owned.
