# Evening 12 — Any Doubts? — The Teens Lead

*FotH Evening · Third movement, Mission and Rhythm · 2027, two weeks after Leading the Room · Anchor: Mark 9:14–29; "I believe; help my unbelief!" (Mark 9:24)*

> **This evening's full page is written before it is walked.** Until then, this page says what the evening does, and the full Companion lesson plan stands behind it: [Getting Started, Week 11 — Any Doubts?](../getting-started/week-11-doubts.md).

*Added at the parents' meeting, 22 November 2026. The full page and its materials are written before the evening.*

**What it does.** The follow-up to December 1. At [Evening 8](session-11-any-doubts.md) John walked the parents through Any Doubts?, and the parents led John. Tonight the teens present the practice and lead the rest of the teens through it, after a planning lab, with the adults close behind. The four steps are the same: Name, Exaggerate, Sit, Re-read.

**Goes home.** The practice once more, with a partner. And find and mark your Tell sentences from Evenings 6–9, ready for Doing the Word.

**Picks up.** Evening 8's practice, seen once, and Evening 11's facilitation, still fresh. Doubts honestly named lead straight into Evening 13, Doing the Word, whose first step is to test what we heard: named, then tested, then done.

## For the leads

- **The bright line holds.** A teen leads the practice as written and hands anything heavy to an adult at once. Accompanying someone's inner life is never asked of a teen.
- **The silent place.** If it moved from Evening 8, it may find its time here; the debrief of December 1 decides.
- **Which teens lead** is settled at the planning lab before the evening.
