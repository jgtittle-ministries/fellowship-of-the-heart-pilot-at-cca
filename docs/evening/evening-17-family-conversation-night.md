# Evening 17 — Family Conversation Night

*FotH Evening · Third movement, Mission and Rhythm · 2027 · Anchor: Acts 2:46*

> **This evening's full page is written before it is walked.** Until then, this page says what the evening does, and the full Companion lesson plan stands behind it: [Getting Started, Week 16 — Family Conversation Night](../getting-started/week-16-family-night.md).

**What it does.** Low teaching, high warmth: a shared meal, a table grace (Acts 2:46), and conversation cards over dessert. The container simply holds us for an evening.

**Goes home.** Keep the Rhythm Card, and talk as a family about a passage to learn by heart.

**Picks up.** The trust the room has built, enjoyed for its own sake.
