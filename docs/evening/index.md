## What this is

FotH Evening is *Fellowship of the Heart* in its home-evening form: the
**Getting Started** journey — the four Connects: Self, Others, God,
Mission — walked around a family table, every two weeks, with **teens
leading the sessions** and adults in the circle as full participants.

Two teens hold each evening: one holds the *container* — the safety and
warmth of the room — and one presents the *content*. The roles rotate as
teens are ready and willing. An adult is always in the circle as the
backstop, and the group walks under pastoral covering, named aloud in the
room.

It is for teens, parents, and standalone adults alike. Every session
stands alone by design: a newcomer or a drop-in lands softly, and the
welcome always includes two minutes of *where we are on the map*.

## What kind of pilot this is

This is a pilot of the **curriculum and of leader training**, not a
measured experiment. The first evenings, in August, made that plain: too
much was still moving, from the materials to the shape of the night to
teens learning to lead, for any measurement to be honest. The pilot needs
two things at once. What works has to be stabilized, and what does not yet
work needs freedom to be tried another way. So every evening is debriefed,
the materials change as the room teaches us (each change is in the
[change log](CHANGELOG.md)), and the question being asked is whether the
journey can be handed on: whether teens can lead it from the page. Whether
and how anyone's growth is ever read comes later, once there is something
steady enough to read.

## The evening, fixed

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 6:00 | Dinner together. The table is part of the container. |
| 7:00 | The session — teaching, practice, and the shared circle, teen-led. |
| 8:30 | Fellowship, and the leadership window: feedback and planning. |
| 9:00 | Done. |

Between evenings, the [two-week take-home](handbook.md#the-two-week-take-home)
carries the practice: a simple rhythm for each week, and a check-in with a
named partner at the end of week one.

The four movements: Evenings 1–5, *Connect with Self and Others*; 6–9,
*Connect with God*; 10–19, *Mission and Rhythm*; 20–23, *Consolidation and
Sending*. Seven evenings belong to the evening form alone, and the school
series has no session for them: Heart Bible Study (9), Spiritual Styles
(10), Leading the Room (11), Any Doubts? led by the teens (12), Doing the
Word (13), Serving Together (15) and Carrying the Word (18). The school series' second runnings A and B are not separate
evenings here: every evening already gives willing teens a full leading rep,
and the rehearsal moved to the planning lab.

[The Whole Road](the-whole-road.md) is the whole plan on one page: what
each of the twenty-three evenings does, what goes home, and how each evening
builds on the ones before it, along three threads: trust, skills, and teen
leadership.

The [Evening Handbook](handbook.md) is the one-sitting guide to how the
evenings work — the shape of the night, the two teen roles, the take-home
rhythm, and what surrounds it all. What has changed, evening by evening, is
in the [change log](CHANGELOG.md).

## The same journey, three expressions

FotH Evening walks the same curriculum as the
[Getting Started series](../getting-started/index.md) of the
[CCA pilot](../cca/index.md), the school-year expression on Wednesday
afternoons. Same journey, different clocks. A third expression,
[Adult FotH](../adult/index.md), walks it with a church's leaders first,
and what the evenings teach is being carried into it. The full Companion lesson plans, the
[Companion Handbook](../getting-started/handbook.md), and the
[shared participant materials](../shared/index.md) all stand behind these
evenings; the pages here carry what the evening form changes.

Before all three comes [the Four Connects](../four-connects.md), the
recommended prequel: six modules that ground heart work in the two greatest
commandments.

Behind both stands the *Intentional Journey of the Heart* — start with
[Start here](../start-here.md) if you want the why underneath the how.
