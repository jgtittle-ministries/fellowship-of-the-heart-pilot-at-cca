## What this is

FotH Evening is *Fellowship of the Heart* in its home-evening form: the
**Getting Started** journey — the four Connects: Self, Others, God,
Mission — walked around a family table, every two weeks, with **teens
leading the sessions** and adults in the circle as full participants.

Two teens hold each evening: one holds the *container* — the safety and
warmth of the room — and one presents the *content*. The roles rotate as
teens are ready and willing. An adult is always in the circle as the
backstop, and the group walks under pastoral covering, named aloud in the
room.

It is for teens, parents, and standalone adults alike. Every session
stands alone by design: a newcomer or a drop-in lands softly, and the
welcome always includes two minutes of *where we are on the map*.

Between evenings, the [two-week take-home](handbook.md#the-two-week-take-home)
carries the practice: a simple rhythm for each week, and a check-in with a
named partner at the end of week one.

## The same journey, two expressions

FotH Evening walks the same curriculum as the
[Getting Started series](../getting-started/index.md) of the CCA pilot —
the school-year expression on Wednesday afternoons. Same journey, two
clocks. The full Companion lesson plans, the
[Companion Handbook](../getting-started/handbook.md), and the
[shared participant materials](../shared/index.md) all stand behind these
evenings; the pages here carry what the evening form changes.

Behind both stands the *Intentional Journey of the Heart* — start with
[Start here](../start-here.md) if you want the why underneath the how.
