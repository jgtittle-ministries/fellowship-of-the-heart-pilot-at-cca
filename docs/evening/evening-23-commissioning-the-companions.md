# Evening 23 — Commissioning the Companions

*FotH Evening · Fourth movement, Consolidation and Sending, its close · Fall 2027 · Anchor: 2 Timothy 2:2; 1 Timothy 4:12*

> **This evening's full page is written before it is walked.** Until then, this page says what the evening does, and the full Companion lesson plan stands behind it: [Getting Started, Week 22 — Commissioning the Companions](../getting-started/week-22-commissioning.md).

**What it does.** Where the whole road has been heading: each person hearing something of their calling and mission, then commissioned, blessed and sent. Each person names what they have heard of their calling so far. The teens who have led are commissioned to lead others (2 Timothy 2:2; 1 Timothy 4:12): they open and close the container from memory, the covering is named, and their first planning lab for leading others is set before anyone leaves.

**Goes home.** For the commissioned, the first lab; for everyone, the rhythm, and the calling heard so far.

**Picks up.** Twenty-two evenings of leading, now handed on. Trust, fully owned.
