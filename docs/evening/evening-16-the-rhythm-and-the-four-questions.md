# Evening 16 — The Rhythm and the Four Questions

*FotH Evening · Third movement, Mission and Rhythm · 2027 · Anchor: Galatians 6:9; Psalm 139:23–24*

> **This evening's full page is written before it is walked.** Until then, this page says what the evening does, and the full Companion lesson plan stands behind it: [Getting Started, Week 15 — The Rhythm and the Four Questions](../getting-started/week-15-rhythm.md).

**What it does.** The serving report from Evening 15 opens the evening. Then each person builds a Rhythm Card: the practices they will keep after the evenings stop (Galatians 6:9). Parents receive four questions to ask their teen at home, if they are willing, and to hear the answers without defending (Psalm 139:23–24).

**Goes home.** Run the Rhythm Card for real; for willing parents, the four questions.

**Picks up.** Every practice so far, now chosen and arranged into a personal rhythm. Evening 3's prayer, "Search me, O God," is now asked between parent and teen.
