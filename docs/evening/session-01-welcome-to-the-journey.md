# Sessions 1–2 — Welcome to the Journey · The Soil of Your Heart

*FotH Evening · the opening of the journey*

**Two sessions, one opening.** Session 1 welcomes a room into the journey
and opens the map; Session 2 turns the first box, *Connect with Self*, into
honest self-knowledge. In August 2026 the founding group walked both in a
single evening. A room with newcomers in it takes them as two evenings,
Session 1 and then Session 2, as set out below.

---

## Session 1 — Welcome to the Journey

*Anchor: John 10:10b; Luke 23:39–43; Romans 10:9–10*

**This evening.** The opening night for a room with newcomers in it. We
establish the container with this specific group, introduce the four
Connects — Self, Others, God, Mission — as the spine of the whole journey,
state the seed plainly and settle what it means to be saved (the thief's
case), and give every person their first taste of what genuine community
feels like.

**Where we are on the map.** Every evening now opens on the Four Connects
map — Self, Others, God, Mission — locating the session on the journey.
Tonight the map itself is the teaching: Session 1 opens all four boxes, a
sequence rooted in the two great commandments (Matt. 22:37–40), and the
kitchen-table block walks it in full.

**Who leads.** The teens who walked the opening sessions in the prototype
group hold the two roles tonight. A newcomer's first sight of this work is
a teenager leading it well: that is the witness, and it is deliberate.

**Bring.** A notebook or journal — we do reflective writing each session.
Clipboards with paper are on hand for anyone who needs them. No pre-work:
you were invited to dinner.

### The take-home (two weeks)

*Printed sheets are handed out in the room; this is the same content.*

**1. The homework practice — week one.** The two standing daily questions. Each
morning, five minutes: "Lord, what are you up to today, and what do you
want me to notice?" Each evening, one journal line: "What did I notice
today?" One box per day, M–Su.

**2. The end-of-week-one check-in.** At the end of week one, connect with
the partner you named at commissioning and report how it's going against
the practice. A text, a call, or a hallway conversation all count. You are
not grading each other; you are expecting each other.

**3. The homework practice — week two.** Keep the morning and evening questions,
and add the abundance question: read John 10:10b once and ask, where is
abundance in my life right now, and where isn't it? Watch for one specific
moment worth telling the circle about.

**4. One sentence of preparation.** Read John 10:10 once more the day
before we meet, and bring one honest question about the journey.

*The journal's standing closer applies from tonight: if I were to teach
this practice to someone, what would I tell them? And a missed box is
never a failure — it is an invitation to return.*

### For the leads

This session's full scripts, watch-fors, and contingencies are in the
school form's [Week 1 Companion lesson plan](../getting-started/week-01-welcome.md);
what follows is what the evening form changes.

- **The home, not the school.** No room-build crew; the household sets the
  circle before dinner. Dinner at six is the arrival window, the
  name-learning, and the first hospitality; by seven, nobody is a stranger
  to the room.
- **The adult backstop** (John or a parent) sits in the circle as a
  participant and holds anything personal that surfaces; the door out is
  named aloud in the welcome, as always.
- **No Personal Heart Journal handed out** — everyone brings their own
  notebook. The Pre-Series Survey is not handed out tonight; it is offered
  family by family in the permission conversations afterward, on each yes.
- **Veterans carry the one-sentence prep** from their handout; newcomers
  have none.
- **A pair leads, visibly.** Name it in the welcome: two leaders hold
  tonight — container and content — and the room will see the evening
  handed back and forth between them. That is the design, not a hiccup.
- **Practice the handoffs beforehand.** The leading pair walks the session
  together before the evening — at the planning lab, or before dinner — so
  each knows who is where in the flow.

**Watch-fors, evening additions.** The school version's watch-fors all
stand (first-night nerves, chaperoning parents, sentimentality,
over-explaining, receiving every saved-answer without grading). Two more
for a home evening:

- **The veterans' shorthand.** The teens who have done this before will be
  tempted to finish newcomers' sentences and skip the beginner steps. The
  planning lab names this; the container holder watches for it.
- **The host-family gravity.** In a family's home, the family's own
  patterns run the room by default. The opening protocol is what hands the
  room to the container instead; do not shortcut it tonight of all nights.

*The note on the 8:20–8:28 stretch: commissioning and the Leader Feedback
Round share it, four minutes each. The leaders' three answers come first;
the room's responses have fit in the same block before — let them, and let
anything longer flow into the 8:30 window.*

## Session 2 — The Soil of Your Heart

*Anchor: Mark 4:1–20; Romans 7:15–25*

**This evening.** The seed stated at Session 1 is recalled, and the Heart
Soil diagnostic from Mark 4, the parable of the Sower and Jesus' own
explanation of it, becomes a frame for honest self-knowledge. Paul's own
mixed ground (Romans 7) is the proof that an honest heart holds all four
soils. This is where *Connect with Self* begins in earnest.

**Bring.** Your notebook or journal, and your take-home from Session 1.

### The take-home (two weeks)

*Printed sheets are handed out in the room; this is the same content.*

**The standing practice — every day, both weeks.** Morning, five minutes:
"Lord, what are you up to today, and what do you want me to notice?"
Evening, one journal line: "What did I notice today?"

**1. The homework practice — week one.** Three times this week, journal:
where did each kind of soil show up today? No fixing. Just noticing.

**2. The end-of-week-one check-in.** At the end of week one, connect with
the partner you named at commissioning and report how it's going against
the practice. You are not grading each other; you are expecting each other.

**3. The homework practice — week two.** Continue the soil-noticing, or
deepen it; journal what happens.

**4. One sentence of preparation.** Set at commissioning.

*A missed box is never a failure — it is an invitation to return.*

### For the leads

This session's full scripts, watch-fors, and crisis contingencies are in
the school form's [Week 2 Companion lesson plan](../getting-started/week-02-soil.md);
read them before the planning lab. What follows is what the evening form
changes.

- **Question zero stays private.** It is journal-only; nobody is asked to
  share it, and nobody should be.
- **Noticing, not fixing.** The diagnostic names the ground; it does not
  prescribe a repair. Keep the room from turning it into a self-improvement
  plan.
- **All four soils in every heart.** Romans 7 is there so no one leaves
  thinking they are simply "the rocky one." Honest hearts hold mixed ground.
- **Pairs at home scale,** and the merge to the container holder. The
  bright line holds; anything of weight goes to the backstop adult.
