# Evening 9 — Heart Bible Study

*FotH Evening · Hearing God together · Anchor: 1 Samuel 3:1–10; Nehemiah 8:8; Acts 13:1–3*

**This evening.** At Evening 6 each of us learned to hear God in Scripture
for ourselves, with PROAPT. Tonight the room learns to hear Him
*together*. Heart Bible Study is PROAPT in its group form: one passage,
read slowly three times, with the room listening for what the Spirit
shows each person, and then for what He shows the group that none of us
heard alone. The passage is the call of the boy Samuel, who learned to
hear God's voice with an older man beside him: "Speak, for your servant
hears" (1 Samuel 3:10).

**Where we are on the map.** This is the first evening back after the
Christmas break, and the last evening of the second movement, *Connect
with God*. Nothing in it is new. Its steps are PROAPT's steps (Evening 6),
and its optional close is the short form of *Any Doubts?* (Evening 8). So
the first night back asks only for practices the room already knows, put
together into one. The full reading of what held over the break comes at
the next evening, [Evening 10 — The Return](../getting-started/week-12-return.md).
Tonight's opening simply welcomes everyone home.

**Why this evening.** Two reasons. First, hearing together is how God has
often spoken to His people: the gathered people listening as the Law was
read "clearly" and its sense given (Nehemiah 8:8), or the church at
Antioch hearing the Spirit while they worshiped together (Acts 13:2).
Second, it is the step Hebrews 5:12–14 asks of us: from being taught, to
teaching others. A teen leads the room's hearing tonight. The next
movement turns outward to Mission, and a group that has learned to hear
together is ready to ask what it is being sent to do.

**Bring.** Your notebook or journal, your Bible, and your take-home from
Evening 8.

## Heart Bible Study — what each step is for

The protocol behind tonight is *Intentional Journey of the Heart*, Volume 5, [A28 — Heart Bible Study with PROAPT](https://jgtittle-ministries.github.io/Intentional-Journey-of-the-Heart/reader.html#docs%2Fvolume-5-references%2Fa28-heart-bible-study-with-proapt.md).

| Step | What the room does | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Pray** | Ask the Spirit to speak. | We come expecting to hear, together. |
| **Read, three times** | Three readers, slowly, eyes closed. | Each reading lands in a different place. Let the passage come to you before you go to it. |
| **Images** | Say what pictures, words or feelings came. | The Spirit often speaks in pictures first. Not everyone thinks in pictures, and that is fine: there is another door. |
| **Same and different** | Notice what others heard. | Often the moment the Spirit shows the group something no one heard alone. |
| **Observe** | What the passage actually says. | The ground under everything else. |
| **Apply** | What the Spirit is highlighting for *me*, tonight. | Where the general word becomes a specific word. |
| **Pray again** | Tell God what you heard and what you will do. | Hearing becomes a response. |
| **Tell** | One sentence to the circle. | Spoken in front of witnesses, the heard word stays. |

## For the two leads

- **Test what is heard.** An Apply impression is weighed, not just
  received: does it agree with Scripture? Does it sound like the Lord's
  conviction, or like accusation? The *How Did You Know?* card from
  Evening 7 has the checks (1 John 4:1; 1 Corinthians 14:29).
- **Keep it a Bible study.** If something heavy surfaces in the Apply step,
  the study does not turn into inner work. It is received, held, and taken
  up afterward with an adult. *This may be therapeutic, but it is not
  therapy.*
- **Don't press.** Some will hear little the first time. A thin answer is
  an honest answer, and the practice works over many evenings, not one.
- **The Tell is the step most often skipped,** and the one that matters
  most. Hold it.
- **If someone heard a truth they don't yet trust,** name *Any Doubts?*
  as a practice to take home with it. Tonight there isn't time to run it
  in the room.

## The take-home (two weeks)

- **Week one:** daily PROAPT, as at Evening 6, at a time of day named
  aloud tonight. Keep your Tell sentence in front of you.
- **End of week one:** the check-in with your partner.
- **Week two:** one Heart Bible Study at your own table, a teen leading,
  on 1 Samuel 3:11–21 or a passage you choose. Three readings, the images,
  same and different, one sentence each.
- **Before The Return:** two sentences in your journal: *the practice I
  kept over the break was ________*, and *the one I most want to keep is ________*.
