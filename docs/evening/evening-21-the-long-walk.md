# Evening 21 — The Long Walk

*FotH Evening · Fourth movement, Consolidation and Sending · 2027 · Anchor: Isaiah 50:10*

> **This evening's full page is written before it is walked.** Until then, this page says what the evening does, and the full Companion lesson plan stands behind it: [Getting Started, Week 20 — The Long Walk](../getting-started/week-20-long-walk.md).

**What it does.** Teens teach the room two mercy cards: the signs of drifting, and the path home. Then the room walks back through its own year, journal in hand, and gathers one-sentence testimonies (Isaiah 50:10).

**Goes home.** Find the two cards at home, and show one family member where they live.

**Picks up.** The whole year, read back in the journal. The teens teach the room.
