# How to read this page

The Getting Started series is one seamless twenty-two-week walk — every week has its own full session plan, numbered in a single sequence from Week 1 to Week 22 (see the [series index](index.md)). This page simply pins each week to its Wednesday on CCA's 2026–27 club calendar: four quarters, 6/5/6/5, beginning September 2, 2026 and ending April 28, 2027. Not every Wednesday is a club Wednesday — the school year breathes around an early-September skip, Thanksgiving, the long Christmas rest, and a February week — and the quarters breathe with it. When a date shifts, change it here — the curriculum does not move.

The club meets **Wednesday afternoons, 4:15–5:30 PM** — seventy-five minutes. The school day releases the room at 4:00; the first fifteen minutes are the room build (team plus a teen crew), with families arriving as it happens. The one exception is Week 21, Sending and Blessing, which runs 4:15–6:15 to hold the family commissioning without hurry — the one night needing the room past 5:30 — the team and the families settle that evening’s logistics as it approaches. (The session plans keep their evening script language — *tonight* — on purpose; only the clock is CCA's.)

# Measurement touchpoints (summary — the full plan is Handbook Section 8)

- **Weekly:** the Three Vital Signs log at every team debrief; the Leader Feedback Round noted (a scheduled weekly instance of costly telling).
- **Quarterly:** the Pulse at Weeks 6, 11, and 16 (distributed at the quarter's close, collected at the next gathering's door); the Post-Series Survey at Week 20.
- **At every re-entry (Weeks 7, 12, 18):** practice survival across the break, Path Home Card uses, time-to-settled-container — the three break experiments.
- **Before any of it:** the permission conversations in the gap after Week 1 — every participant-facing page (the before-and-after and the three Pulses) is asked for, family by family, and handed over only on a yes (Handbook Section 8).
- **Outside reads:** invited Walk-In Readings under the covering's authority (vetted reader, blind to content, private report), once or twice a quarter at ordinary weeks — never at ceremonies.

# The two gates this calendar watches

1. **Student leadership in writing** (CCA legal review, in motion): needed before Week 4 (Sep 30) for the full teen-led Story II — adult-led with teen assisting until then; the series runs either way.
2. **Commissioning at year's end** (Week 22): the FC1 exit gate per Handbook Section 11 — a sending witnessed by the whole cohort, with each commissioned Companion's covering named and, for a graduating senior, the referral card built for the campus they are actually headed to.
