# Design Status

*How the design is faring: updated after each debrief, at the level of the room*

This page carries forward what the article "If a Teenager Can Use It…" describes as of one date. It holds four things: where the evening series stands, how each of the article's conjectures is faring, the tally of changes and where they came from, and the decisions that shaped the design, each with its date and its reason.

Three rules govern what appears here. Nothing names a participant or describes any one person; the room is one family, so even a pattern is written for the room as a whole. Nothing here is a result: these are observations, read in practices and the room, never in a person. And when a count or question changes, the new one starts over and is never compared with the old ([How the evenings are watched](../evening/handbook.md)).

**Last updated:** 25 November 2026, after the Evening 7 debrief and the parents' meeting of 22 November.

## Where the series stands

- **Planned:** twenty-three evenings in four movements ([The Whole Road](../evening/the-whole-road.md)), about every two weeks, monthly in the summer, running into the fall of 2027.
- **Walked:** seven. Evenings 1 to 3 in August, with the founding group; then Evening 4 on 15 September, Evening 5 on 7 October (closing the first movement), Evening 6 on 26 October and Evening 7 on 10 November.
- **Next:** Evening 8 on 1 December, after the Thanksgiving break; then the Christmas break, and Evening 9 in January.
- **Guests:** two at Evening 5 and two at Evening 7. Guests are not part of the pilot: they sign nothing, and none is named in any record. What they say comes back through the person who invited them ([Newcomers, drop-ins, and guests](../evening/handbook.md)).

## The change tally

Every change to the evenings is logged with its date and its reason in the [Evening change log](../evening/CHANGELOG.md). For the tally, each substantive change is sorted by where it came from.

- **From the room:** something tried in an evening, its debrief or the planning lab showed it was needed, even if John was the only one who saw it. Also any proposal, from anyone in the group, John included, that the parents and teens talked over and agreed to.
- **From John's review:** his own review, idea or source material, with no event in the room behind it. "John's word" on a log entry means he approved it, not that it was his idea.
- **Left out:** dates, renumbering, materials built to a design already decided, site and menu work, typo and clock fixes, and ripples, which are counted once, where they began.

| Period | From the room | From John's review |
|---|---|---|
| 16–21 September, after Evening 4 | 14 | 7 |
| 2–8 October, around Evening 5 | 7 | 6 |
| 11–17 October, planning lab, no evening | 5 | 11 |
| 27 October–7 November, after Evening 6 | 9 | 5 |
| 11 November, after Evening 7 | 6 | 1 |
| 12–16 November, no evening | 0 | 5 |
| 17–23 November, the parents' meeting | 10 | 2 |
| **Total** | **51** | **37** |

Most of John's thirty-seven went to evenings not yet walked, which no room can yet speak to. That is why the split is not yet support for C7: the test is read at the end of the series, on evenings already walked through. As the parents and teens take a growing part in choosing, shaping and sequencing what follows, the room's share is expected to grow.

## Decisions that shaped the design

A few of the decisions in the log, chosen because they changed how the evenings run or how they are watched. The log has them all.

| Date | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Before the year | The signs of growth rewritten | Field-tested on two adult groups, a men's Bible study and the first Four Connects workshop, they read a healthy young room as a failing mature one. |
| 16 September | Pairs, not clusters, in the practice block | The first debrief: timing was a challenge every session. |
| 16 September | The series condensed and renumbered | The numbering left over from the school version confused the room. |
| 8 October | Leader notes color-coded by role | A teen leader asked for it after Evening 5; every evening's notes were rebuilt within the week. |
| 14 October | Evenings 1 and 2 become two evenings again | Walked together in August, they were too crowded. |
| 28 October | Breaks and returns set by the holidays | Evenings every two weeks have no school quarters; the parents set the calendar. The question on return: did you come back to it, all by yourself, because you wanted to? |
| 28 October | The quarterly Pulse dropped from the evening form | The read became the return round at each long break and a quiet count of which practices people chose, with no names. |
| Early November | Four evenings added: Heart Bible Study, Doing the Word, Serving Together, Carrying the Word | Drafted by John and accepted by the parents. |
| 11 November | Keeping the evening to time: the homework talked over at dinner | Whole-room rounds grow with every person added, and the evenings were running long; the practice keeps its full time. |
| 16 November | The referral list moved into the Evening Handbook, with a counselor added | The list lived only in the school version's handbook, and it had no counselor for what needs more care than an evening can give. |
| 22 November | Twenty-three evenings, accepted at a meeting with the parents and teens | Three evenings added (Spiritual Styles, Leading the Room, Any Doubts? led by the teens), the order agreed. |
| 22 November | One question about home, at every dinner | The parents' weeks are full and they were not keeping track, so three written questions became one spoken question, asked the same way each time. |
